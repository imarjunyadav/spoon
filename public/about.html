Our Story

Spoon was born from a simple idea: make canteen ordering fast, easy, and hassle-free. We noticed students and staff spending too much time waiting in long queues, especially during peak hours. So we built Spoon - a smart ordering app that lets you browse thee menu, place orders, and pick up your food without the wait.

Our Mission

To revolutionize the canteen experience by providing a seamless, efficient, and delightful way to order food. We believe your lunch break should be about enjoying your meal, not waiting in line.
