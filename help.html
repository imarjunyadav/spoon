Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse the menu, add items to your cart, and click "Proceed to Checkout". You can choose to order now or schedule for later.

What are your operating hours?

We're open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM every day. Pre-orders can be scheduled within these hours.

How long does it take to prepare my order?

Most orders are ready within 10-15 minutes. You'll receive a notification when your order is ready for pickup.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, you can cancel your order before it's marked as "Preparing". Contact us immediately if you need to cancel.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major payment methods through Razorpay including UPI, credit/debit cards, and net banking.
